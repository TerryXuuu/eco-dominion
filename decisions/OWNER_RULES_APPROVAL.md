## Exceptions or additional rulings

No additional gameplay amendment is recommended beyond the 26 rulings and their clarifications above.

- **Choice ordering:** active player then opponent for shared-event choices; opponent then active player for simultaneous shared-event hand overflow. Treat these as distinct queues and test both.
- **Stability activation:** evaluate lizard activation using base stability plus deer contributions before adding lizard contributions. Recompute on migration or suppression; do not accumulate refresh bonuses.
- **Settlement order:** derive pre-penalty control, pay the active side’s applicable income, apply each qualifying controller’s quarter-power penalty once, recompute control, check active-player domination, and only then perform final scoring when applicable.
- **Network policy:** R26 approves no unspecified timeout algorithm by implication. Exact deterministic choices, timeout counter resets and outage handling still need an explicit executable specification and tests.
- **Versioning:** after actual approval, freeze a versioned specification with a content hash and meaningful regression fixtures. Preserve the draft and original sources; existing matches retain their original ruleset. Do not use the preparatory source hashes below as the approved ruleset hash.
